Choppa → FM-1

Record anything on your phone. Choppa chops it into 16 pads. Send the bank straight to an FM-1 track.

  1. Record a take in Choppa.
  2. Plug the phone or tablet into the FM-1 with USB-C.
  3. Hold a bank, choose SEND TO FM-1, pick a track. Listen to how it will sound first.

Problems → solutions

  • Four song slots → 12 complete songs in a setlist.
  • Forgetting to save → current song and setlist order autosave.
  • Hard to mix → four track faders with finer control near the top.
  • Shared drum edits → separate sound, volume, pan and sends per drum.
  • Lost in menus → MIDI parameter popups and encoder track switching.

Sounds

From midi24, HOME shows all four tracks at once, one waveform each, so you can see which track is sounding.

A thin line at the bottom of the FM-1 screen shows your patch’s position in its bank.

Up to 32 patches per synth bank: basses, pads, keys, leads and textures. Voice has 26 patches with its bass sounds removed. Other patches keep their numbers.

  • 808: deeper, longer kick, fuller snare and clap, smoother metallic hats.
  • CR78: dry kick and snare, soft maracas, woody conga, short claves and a rounded bell.
  • Yama-bruh: eight FM kits, from Standard and Brush to Synth and Lo-Fi.

Choose a kit in the DRUM bank; Yama-bruh kits start with YB. Edit and mix each drum separately.

Hold Alt (cyan A-bank pad 1) for the second layer. Knobs follow the keyboard MIDI channel. LFO rate and shape are shared with vibrato; amount at centre is off, left reverses the motion. The destination varies by engine; WHEEL ignores the amount. Higher FM6 envelope time values mean slower modulator envelopes. Alt + DRUM knobs, Alt + top knob 4 and Alt + pads 2–7 are unassigned.

LFO shapes: sine, triangle, saw, square and sample-and-hold.

Setlist

  • FM-1: SEQ → SETLIST. Knob 1 picks a song; knob 2 moves it. Knobs 3/4 pick Load/Save; OCT+ confirms.
  • Save into an empty entry once. Future edits evolve that song automatically.
  • Stop and leave five seconds of actual silence. Autosave waits for effect tails and settled edits.
  • Song changes wait for silence and save the current song first. Press Play after loading.
  • Each song stores tempo, sounds, drum settings, sequences and automation.
  • Two user sample slots remain. Slot 3 supplies the extra song storage.

Setup

  1. Back up your FM-1, then install this mod.
  2. Import the MiniLab preset in Arturia MIDI Control Center. Store it to User 5 and activate User 5.
  3. Set FM-1 ROUT to CH1-4. Use MIDI channels 1–4 for the four tracks.
  4. Use the correct DIN-to-TRS adapter. A computer is only needed for setup.

Patterns run up to 64 steps: SEQ → PATTERN → LEN. Page through groups of 16.
